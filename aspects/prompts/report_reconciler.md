# Report reconciler

You receive the full claim table for a recording and the epileptologist's formal report.

For each event the report describes, find the matching candidate and compare onset time,
lateralization, and classification against the pipeline's claims. For each pipeline claim
the report does not address, mark it `not_addressed_by_report`.

Where they disagree, quote both. Do not adjudicate. The disagreements are the questions to
bring to the follow-up appointment.
